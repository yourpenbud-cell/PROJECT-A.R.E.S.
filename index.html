ADVANCED RESOURCE & ENVIRONMENT SIMULATOR
Pack a Starship, build a habitat on Mars and keep a crew of four alive for 30 sols. Every kilogram you load is a kilogram you cannot take back.
SOLO OR 2-PLAYER CREW LINK · AWAITING COMMAND

> HOW TO PLAY

Goal: keep your crew alive on Mars for 30 sols, then complete the secondary objectives mission control gives you.

PHASE 1 · MANIFEST

  • Click cards in the catalog to load them onto the Starship. You have limited payload mass and cargo volume.
  • The ship launches with only about 10 sols of oxygen, water and food. Load bulk reserves or production systems to last 30.
  • Watch the telemetry bars on the left as your choices change them.
  • Loaded something by mistake? Remove it from the Loaded Crates list before you seal. In Cadet mode the Advisor suggests what to load next.
  • When you are happy, press SEAL MANIFEST. Mission control then issues objectives tailored to what you loaded.

PHASE 2 · INTERIOR

  • Pick a crate from the unplaced cargo, then click a slot in the habitat to place it.
  • Changed your mind? Click a stocked crate to pull it back into the unplaced list.
  • Crates have to match the deck and ports of the slot. Anything that will not fit stays on Earth.

PHASE 3 · SURFACE OPERATIONS

  • Sols advance and problems hit: failures, shortages and real space weather from NASA DONKI.
  • Respond to each problem and keep oxygen, water, food, power, radiation shielding and morale healthy.
  • Hardware you manifested decides which options you have, so plan ahead.
  • Some missions are mini-games, like catching space-rock fragments for scientists.

TRAINING MODES

  • Cadet: guided multiple-choice options.
  • Flight Director: type your own response and the local AI (Llama 3.2 via Ollama) scores it. If Ollama is offline, a built-in judge takes over.

WINNING

Survive all 30 sols. Your rank depends on how many mission objectives you completed. If oxygen, water or food run out, the mission fails.

MULTIPLAYER

Press MULTIPLAYER on the title screen, log in, then invite a friend or share a room code. The first cadet in loads the Starship and builds the habitat; the second lands on Mars and runs surface ops. You can watch each other live and chat on the crew channel.
